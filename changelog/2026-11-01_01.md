# The Agent-Harnesses Study's Profiles Become a Bundle Any Agent Can Read

## Why Care?

`agent-harnesses/context-v/` holds the source-cited profiles of the ten harnesses pinned in this study (Aider, AutoGen, Cline, Codex, Continue, Goose, the MCP Python SDK, OpenCode, OpenHands, Pi) and the inquiry that answers the study's targeted questions from them. Now the folder is an Open Knowledge Format (OKF) v0.2 bundle, so any OKF reader can list the profiles and what each one found without opening them.

## What's New?

- **All 11 docs declare their `type`**, the Train-Case name of their folder: `Profiles` (10) and `Inquiry` (1).
- **`context-v/index.md`** marks the bundle with `okf_version: "0.2"` and lists its two folders.
- **Two folder indexes**, one line per doc with its lede.

## Under the Hood

- Part of the tree-wide OKF sweep; see the Context Vigilance Kit's plan, [[Retrofit-Every-Repo-to-OKF-Then-the-Kit-Frontmatter]].
- Every edit was made by hand, one file at a time: 11 added `type:` lines and nothing removed. No dates moved; this is a compliance change, not a content change.
- The pinned upstream repos beside `context-v/` are their own repos and were not touched.
- This is the study's first `changelog/` entry.
